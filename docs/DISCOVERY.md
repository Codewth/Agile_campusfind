## Initial solution-neutral problem statement (provisional)

Students and staff on a college campus may rely on scattered channels for reporting and recovering belongings, including group messages and direct requests to campus security. When reporting is fragmented, it can be difficult to discover a possible matching report or know where an object is being held. Students may spend unnecessary time looking for items, while staff may lack a shared way to check reports. The underlying need is a trustworthy, easy-to-access campus lost-and-found record; the exact scale and process should be validated with students and staff.

## Stakeholders and initial context

Primary participants: students and hostel residents. Other relevant stakeholders: security staff, wardens, library and cafeteria staff, student representatives. Example campus locations: classrooms, library, cafeteria, labs, sports areas, hostels.

## Open questions needing validation

1. Which campus locations have the most reports, and where do members currently report objects?
2. Who is authorized to verify ownership and approve return? How is an ID card handled differently?
3. What details and contact methods are permitted to be public under campus policy?
4. How long should items be stored, how should reports expire, and who manages disputes?
5. Does campus security agree to use the system and what permissions would it need?
